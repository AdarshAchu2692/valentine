My Dearest Love,

I write these words as though pressing my heart onto this paper, hoping the ink might carry even a fraction of what I feel for you. From the day you entered my life, everything found its meaning.

Even when we argue, even when silence lingers between us, my love never fades. Nammal adi idum, mindathe irikkum, enth paranjaalum — my heart chooses you, always.

Enikku nee illathe pattilla. You are my one and only, the quiet strength behind my smiles, the warmth I return to every time.

I promise to grow kinder, stronger, and better — to love you sincerely, faithfully, and without end.

Forever Yours,
